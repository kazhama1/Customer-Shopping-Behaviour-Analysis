03 / Methodology

How the featured project works.

The workflow connects data preparation with two data mining techniques.

STEP 01

Prepare

Load the retail dataset, remove duplicates, handle missing customer details and filter invalid transactions.

STEP 02

Explore

Summarise sales, popular products, customer spending and monthly sales patterns.

STEP 03

Segment

Use RFM features and K-Means clustering to group customers by purchasing behaviour.

STEP 04

Discover

Use Apriori to identify frequent itemsets and generate product association rules.

Unsupervised learning

K-Means clustering

Groups customers using Recency, Frequency and Monetary (RFM) features. The code scales log-transformed customer features and evaluates the clustering with a silhouette score.

Association rule mining

Apriori algorithm

Finds frequently occurring product combinations in transactions and derives association rules using support, confidence and lift.

Exploratory analysis

Data visualisation

Uses charts to inspect product popularity, customer spending distribution, monthly sales and customer clusters.
